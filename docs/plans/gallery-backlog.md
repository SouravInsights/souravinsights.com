## Beenthere · Mobile delete interaction

The trip card's swipe-to-delete only appears on mobile, so it never showed up
in the desktop recordings. A swipe lefts the card, a red bin grows and rotates
into view, and a pop fires once you cross the threshold.

Needs a fresh phone recording.

**Beenthere Mobile Delete Interaction**

> Deleting a trip should never be an accident, so it takes a real swipe. Drag
> the card left and a red bin grows and rotates into view, and once you cross
> the line it gives a little pop in your hand. Let go too early and it springs
> back like nothing happened. A fast flick works too, for people who've done it
> before. It's meant to feel like pulling a folder off a shelf and deciding
> it's done, not tapping a delete button.

Note: the desktop card prefetches the trip on hover. That detail belongs to the
trip cards clip, not this one.
