How It Works

Add your repo.

Detail will clone it to a secure sandbox and confirm we can build your code properly.

Detail exercises your code.

Detail runs thousands of checks and diagnostics, looking for bugs and selecting the most important ones.

Receive fixes.

Detail sends you a PR with a fix. Or, we can deliver a ticket and you can fix it yourself.

Keep your Codebase Sane

Dead Code

Agents leave behind piles of dead code. We clean it up for you.

Internal Documentation

Your agents depend on skills and READMEs that nobody is paying attention to. We maintain them for you.

Batteries Included but Removable

Bring your own Factory

Get bug fixes in Detail, or fix them in your cloud dev environment of choice. Customize prompts, or we can handle it end-to-end.

Meets you where you are

Not ready for a self-healing codebase? We can send tickets to your Linear, Jira, or Asana and assign them to whoever has the most context.

Enterprise Friendly

SOC 2 Type II Certified

Highest standards of compliance with industry best practices. Audit report available upon request.

Zero Data Retention

All code and usage data can be purged based on your requirements. Our model providers retain nothing.
