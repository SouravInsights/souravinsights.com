Who is the deck for?+

Founders, developers, product managers, and marketers. They all use the deck to weave in the emotional triggers that make up winning app experiences.

What's inside?+

Plays: individual tactics. There are 33 of them, and each play is five cards: the tactic card, a Make It Yours prompt card, and three real example cards.
Stacks: groups of plays that focus on a single area like onboarding, activation, or retention.

There are 12 stacks in total:

Why a deck of cards?+

It's an active working tool to be shuffled, combined and remixed. There isn't a linear path to follow.

The deck is a digital download only.

Do I need design experience?+

No. You don't need to know how to design visually. Each of the 33 tactic cards comes with 3 sourced examples from real apps, 99 visual examples in total, showing you exactly how the strategy looks when it's done well.

Will this work for the app in my niche?+

Yes. The emotional layer applies to all apps, web or mobile. The “Make it Yours” cards help you tailor to your specific scenario.

Use this before or after the launch?+

Both. Before launch, it helps you build the right flows from day one so you're not patching problems later. After launch, it helps you diagnose what's not working and fix it with something proven instead of guessing.
