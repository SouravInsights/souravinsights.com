Cost Under Control - No Surprises

Dash0’s transparent pricing ensures you always know what you’re paying for—no hidden fees, no guesswork, just predictable costs.

Price by telemetry

Pay just for the telemetry you care about

Our pricing is transparent and based on how many logs, spans, and metric data points you send and store. Not how many bytes of it, or how many people query them. This ensures that you only pay for what you use and can send all the metadata that you need, without any hidden costs associated with users or GB. With SignalControl, storage is billed only on the telemetry you keep.

Open standards

Built on the OpenTelemetry Collector

SignalControl works with the OpenTelemetry agents and collectors you already run - no proprietary agent, no gateway tier to build. Prefer to manage your own pipelines? Everything stays standard OTel, and otelbin.io is there to help.
